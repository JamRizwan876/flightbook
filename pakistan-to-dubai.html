Important Travel Tips

  • Passport: Check your passport validity before travelling.
  • Visa: Check the latest UAE entry and visa requirements for your circumstances through official channels.
  • Flight ticket: Confirm the departure airport, baggage allowance, flight timings and refund conditions.
  • Accommodation: Arrange suitable accommodation before your trip.
  • Airport arrival: Follow your airline's guidance on when to arrive at the airport.
  • Budget: Plan for transport, food, accommodation and other travel expenses.

Travel rules can change. Always verify visa and entry requirements with official UAE authorities or the relevant airline before departure.
